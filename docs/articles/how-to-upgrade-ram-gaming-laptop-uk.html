How to Upgrade RAM in a Gaming Laptop (UK Guide)

Running out of memory? A RAM upgrade is the cheapest meaningful upgrade for a gaming laptop — often under £40. Here's how to do it safely.

Step 1: Check if your RAM is upgradeable

Some thin laptops (Zephyrus G14, Blade 16 on some configs) have soldered RAM that can't be upgraded. Check your model's spec sheet or our buying guide first. Most 15-16 inch models have two SODIMM slots.

Step 2: Buy the right memory

Match the generation (DDR4 vs DDR5) and speed (e.g. DDR5-4800). For dual-channel performance, buy a matched pair — 2x8GB or 2x16GB. Reputable UK sellers: Amazon UK, Crucial UK, Box.

Step 3: Install it

Power off, unplug, and remove the bottom panel (usually 8-12 Phillips screws). Ground yourself, release the clips, slot the new sticks at 45°, press down until they click. Boot and verify in Task Manager.

How much RAM do you need?

16GB is the 2026 baseline for gaming. 32GB helps for video editing, VMs and heavy multitasking — but won't raise FPS.

FAQs

Will upgrading RAM void my warranty?

On most brands, no — RAM and SSD are considered user-upgradeable. Keep the original sticks in case of a warranty claim.

Does more RAM increase FPS?

Going from 8GB to 16GB can help; beyond 16GB there's no gaming benefit, but multitasking improves.
