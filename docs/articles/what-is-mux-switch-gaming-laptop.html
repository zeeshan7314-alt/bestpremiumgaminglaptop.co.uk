What is a MUX Switch in a Gaming Laptop? (Explained)

You've seen 'MUX switch' on spec sheets. It's a small chip that can add 5-15% gaming performance — here's how it works and when it matters.

The problem it solves

Normally your dedicated GPU sends frames through the integrated GPU to reach the display — adding latency and costing ~5-10% performance. A MUX (multiplexer) switch lets the dGPU talk directly to the display.

How much difference does it make?

Typically 5-15% more FPS, biggest at 1080p and in CPU-bound games. At 1440p+ the gap shrinks.

NVIDIA Advanced Optimus

The automatic version — switches intelligently without a reboot. Found on most 2024+ premium laptops, including our top picks.

Do you need it?

Nice to have, not essential. Don't pick an inferior laptop just for a MUX switch — but between two equal machines, take the one that has it.

FAQs

Does MUX switch affect battery life?

Yes — direct GPU mode uses more power. Advanced Optimus solves this by switching automatically.

How do I enable the MUX switch?

In Armoury Crate (ASUS), Legion Zone (Lenovo) or the NVIDIA Control Panel — look for 'dGPU only' or 'Ultimate' mode.
