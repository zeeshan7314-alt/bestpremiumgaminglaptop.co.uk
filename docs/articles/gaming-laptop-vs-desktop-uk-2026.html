Gaming Laptop vs Desktop in the UK (2026) — Which Should You Buy?

The eternal debate. We compare on performance per pound, upgradeability, and real UK prices — no fanboyism.

Performance per pound

Desktops win clearly: a £1,200 desktop beats a £1,600 laptop with the 'same' GPU, because desktop chips run at much higher wattage. Expect 20-35% more frames from the desktop.

Total cost of ownership

Desktops can be upgraded piecemeal (new GPU in 3 years); laptops must be replaced whole. Over 6 years, the desktop is usually cheaper despite the higher upfront cost with monitor and peripherals.

When a laptop wins

You move between home/uni/work. You have no desk space. You need one machine for everything. Portability is the laptop's killer feature — desktops can't compete.

The UK price reality

Prebuilt desktops from UK builders (PCSpecialist, AWD-IT) narrow the DIY gap. Factor in a £200-300 monitor for the desktop side.

Our verdict

Stationary gamer? Desktop. Student, commuter, or small flat? A laptop like the Legion Pro 5 is the smart buy.

FAQs

Is a £1500 gaming laptop as good as a £1500 desktop?

No — the desktop will be ~25% faster. You're paying for portability.

Do gaming laptops last as long as desktops?

Typically 3-5 years vs 5-8 for desktops, due to heat and non-upgradeable GPUs.
