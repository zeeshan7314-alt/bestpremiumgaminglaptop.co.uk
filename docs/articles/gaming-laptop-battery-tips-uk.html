Gaming Laptop Battery Life: 9 Tips to Make It Last Longer

Gaming laptops aren't known for battery life — but these tips can take you from 3 hours to 6+ of real work, and keep the battery healthy for years.

1. Use iGPU mode

Force integrated graphics for desktop work (Advanced Optimus does this automatically). This alone can double battery life.

2. Lower refresh rate on battery

Drop from 240Hz to 60Hz when unplugged — the display is a huge power draw.

3. Cap battery charge at 80%

Most manufacturers offer a battery health mode. Keeping lithium at 80% dramatically extends lifespan if you're usually plugged in.

4. Quiet/Silent power plan

Manufacturer silent modes cut CPU wattage massively for browsing and documents.

5. Reduce brightness

The screen is the #1 drain. 50% brightness is plenty indoors.

6. Kill background apps

Discord, Chrome and launchers all sip power constantly.

7. Disable keyboard RGB on battery

Pretty, but thirsty.

8. Undervolt if supported

Less heat = less fan = less power.

9. Replace when degraded

A £60-100 replacement battery at year 3 beats buying a new laptop.

FAQs

Why does my gaming laptop only last 2 hours?

The dGPU is probably active. Switch to iGPU mode and check what's draining power in Task Manager.

Is it bad to leave it plugged in?

Modern laptops manage this well, especially with an 80% charge cap enabled.
