How to Upgrade SSD Storage in a Gaming Laptop

Modern games are 100GB+ each — 512GB fills fast. Adding a second NVMe SSD is easy and cheap. Here's the full process.

Check your slots

Most gaming laptops have two M.2 slots (one occupied). Check the manual — some budget models have only one.

Pick the right SSD

Any PCIe Gen 4 NVMe M.2 2280 SSD works. 1TB is the sweet spot (~£60-80 in the UK); 2TB if you hoard games. Samsung 990 Pro, WD Black SN850X and Crucial T705 are safe bets.

Clone or fresh install?

For a second drive: just slot it in and format in Disk Management. To replace the boot drive, clone with Macrium Reflect (free) or Samsung Magician, then swap.

Don't forget the heatsink

Gen 4 SSDs run hot — use the laptop's thermal pad or a thin graphene heatsink if there's clearance.

FAQs

Can I add a second SSD to any gaming laptop?

Most 15-16 inch models have a spare M.2 slot. Thin 14-inch models often don't — check first.

Will a faster SSD improve gaming FPS?

No, but load times shrink dramatically versus old SATA drives.
