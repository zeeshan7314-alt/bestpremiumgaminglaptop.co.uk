Best Time to Buy a Gaming Laptop in the UK (2026 Calendar)

Timing your purchase can save £200-500. Here's the UK sale calendar ranked by discount depth.

1. Black Friday (late November)

The deepest discounts — £200-500 off premium models. Start watching prices 2 weeks early.

2. Amazon Prime Day (July + October)

Strong on ASUS, Acer and Lenovo models. The October event is now a genuine second Prime Day.

3. Boxing Day / January sales

Retailers clear stock — great for last-gen models at 20-30% off.

4. New GPU launches

When new GPUs launch, previous-gen laptops drop 15-25%. Buy last-gen for the best value.

5. Back to school (August-September)

Student-focused deals and bundles — good for sub-£1,000 machines.

Worst times to buy

Right after launch (full RRP) and mid-summer when stock is thin.

FAQs

Should I wait for Black Friday?

If it's more than 6 weeks away and you can wait, usually yes — savings average £200+.

Do prices drop on Prime Day?

Yes — particularly ASUS and Acer models on Amazon UK.
