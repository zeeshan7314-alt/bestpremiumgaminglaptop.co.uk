With a gaming index of 78/100, the Acer Predator Helios Neo 16 sits in the upper mid-range of our rankings. The NVIDIA RTX 4060 105W handles modern AAA titles at 1080p high settings comfortably, and DLSS 3 frame generation adds meaningful headroom in supported games.
