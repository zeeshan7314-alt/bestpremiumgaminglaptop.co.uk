Performance

With a gaming index of 81/100, the Alienware x14 R2 sits in the upper mid-range of our rankings. The NVIDIA RTX 4060 105W handles modern AAA titles at 1080p high settings comfortably, and DLSS 3 frame generation adds meaningful headroom in supported games.

Display & Design

The 14" QHD 165Hz panel is well matched to the GPU's power. Build quality is excellent — this feels like a true flagship.

Pros

  • Stunning thin design
  • Premium materials throughout
  • Good QHD 165Hz display
  • Portable at 1.9kg

Cons

  • Very expensive for RTX 4060
  • Performance per pound is poor
  • Runs warm

Our Verdict

The x14 R2 is the most beautiful 14-inch gaming laptop ever made — and you pay for it. Performance is solid but not class-leading for £1,899; you're buying design, materials and that alien head logo.
