With a gaming index of 85/100, the Gigabyte Aorus 15 sits in the upper mid-range of our rankings. The NVIDIA RTX 4070 140W handles modern AAA titles at 1440p high/ultra settings comfortably, and DLSS 3 frame generation adds meaningful headroom in supported games.
