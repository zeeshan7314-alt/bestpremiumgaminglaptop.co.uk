Performance

With a gaming index of 90/100, the Lenovo Legion Pro 5 (2026) sits among the fastest laptops we've ranked. The NVIDIA RTX 4070 140W handles modern AAA titles at 1440p high/ultra settings comfortably, and DLSS 3 frame generation adds meaningful headroom in supported games.
