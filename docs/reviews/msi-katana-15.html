With a gaming index of 62/100, the MSI Katana 15 sits at the affordable end of our premium roundup. The NVIDIA RTX 4050 105W handles modern AAA titles at 1080p high settings comfortably, and DLSS 3 frame generation adds meaningful headroom in supported games.
