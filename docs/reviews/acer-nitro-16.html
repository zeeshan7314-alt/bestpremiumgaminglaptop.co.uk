Performance

With a gaming index of 64/100, the Acer Nitro 16 sits at the affordable end of our premium roundup. The NVIDIA RTX 4050 100W handles modern AAA titles at 1080p high settings comfortably, and DLSS 3 frame generation adds meaningful headroom in supported games.

Display & Design

The 16" FHD 165Hz panel is well matched to the GPU's power. Build quality is solid for the price, with no major flex or creaks.

Our Verdict
