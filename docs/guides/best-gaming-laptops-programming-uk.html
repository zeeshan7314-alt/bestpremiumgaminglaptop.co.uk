Buying Tips

Keyboard is king

Legion's keyboards are the best for long typing sessions. Avoid mushy budget keyboards.

16:10 displays

Taller screens show more code. The Zephyrus and Transcend's panels are ideal.

FAQs

Do programmers need a gaming laptop?

Not necessarily — but if you also game or do ML/game-dev, one machine beats two.

MacBook vs gaming laptop for coding?

MacBooks win on battery and Unix tools; gaming laptops win on price-to-performance and GPU compute.
