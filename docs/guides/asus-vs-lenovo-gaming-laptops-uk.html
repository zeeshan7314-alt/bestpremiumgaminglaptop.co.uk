Buying Tips

Thermals

Both are excellent. Legion's cooling is slightly quieter; ROG pushes higher sustained clocks.

UK support

Lenovo's UK on-site warranty options are superb. ASUS support is good but RMA centres are fewer.

Value

Lenovo usually gives you more spec per pound; ASUS charges a premium for design and features like per-key RGB.

FAQs

Which is better: ASUS ROG or Lenovo Legion?

For raw value, Legion wins — similar performance for less money. For premium features and design, ROG edges ahead.

Which brand is more reliable?

Both rank highly in reliability surveys. Buy based on the specific model's thermals and the warranty offered.
