Buying Tips

Display first

100% DCI-P3 and factory calibration matter more than refresh rate for creative work.

NVIDIA for rendering

CUDA and OptiX acceleration make RTX laptops far faster than AMD in Blender, Premiere and DaVinci.

FAQs

How much RAM for 4K video editing?

32GB recommended — the Stealth 16 Studio includes it.

Is OLED good for photo editing?

Excellent — perfect blacks and wide gamut, but calibrate it for print work.
