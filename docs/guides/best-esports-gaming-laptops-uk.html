Buying Tips

Refresh rate > resolution

For esports, a 1080p 240Hz panel beats a 4K 60Hz panel every time. Our top picks all run 165Hz+.

CPU matters too

Valorant and CS2 are CPU-heavy. HX-series processors (i9-14900HX, Ryzen 7745HX) hold high frame rates when it counts.

FAQs

What FPS do I need for esports?

144fps minimum to match a 144Hz display; 240fps+ if you own a 240Hz panel. All our picks exceed 200fps in Valorant/CS2 at competitive settings.

Is a budget laptop enough for esports?

Yes — the Katana 15 and TUF A15 push 200+ fps in Valorant and Fortnite at 1080p for under £950.
