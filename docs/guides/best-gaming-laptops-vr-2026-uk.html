Buying Tips

Minimum vs recommended

Minimum: RTX 4060. Recommended: RTX 4070+. Ideal: RTX 4080 for high-refresh VR.

Don't forget the ports

Quest Link needs USB-C with DP alt-mode; Index needs full DisplayPort or USB-C DP.

FAQs

Can I use PSVR2 with a laptop?

Yes, with Sony's PC adapter — but you'll want RTX 4070+ for a good experience.

Is laptop VR as good as desktop VR?

Close — a full-power RTX 4080 laptop matches a desktop 4070 Ti in VR workloads.
