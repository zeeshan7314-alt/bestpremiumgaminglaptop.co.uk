How to Choose a Gaming Laptop (UK Buying Guide)

GPU names lie. Two laptops both called "RTX 4070" can differ by 30% in real performance. Here's what actually matters — in plain English.

1. The GPU — and its wattage (TGP)

The graphics card decides gaming performance. But the same GPU at different wattages performs differently: a 140W RTX 4070 beats a 100W RTX 4070 by a wide margin. Always check TGP in the spec sheet — if a retailer hides it, that's a red flag.

2026 GPU tiers: RTX 4050 (1080p) → RTX 4060 (1080p high / 1440p medium) → RTX 4070 (1440p high) → RTX 4080 (1440p ultra / 4K).

2. VRAM

8GB is the minimum for 1440p in 2026; 12GB (RTX 4070+) is safer for future games. Avoid 6GB cards for AAA gaming.

3. CPU

Any modern i7/Ryzen 7 HX-series chip is plenty for gaming. Don't pay extra for i9 unless you also do video editing or 3D work.

4. Display

Match the panel to the GPU: 1080p 144Hz+ for RTX 4050/4060; QHD 165Hz+ for RTX 4070/4080. Check brightness (300+ nits) if you'll use it near windows.

5. RAM & Storage

16GB DDR5 and 1TB SSD is the 2026 baseline. Prefer models with upgradeable RAM and a spare M.2 slot.

6. Thermals & Noise

Thin laptops throttle. Read reviews for sustained (not just peak) performance and fan noise — you'll live with both daily.

7. Battery & Portability

Gaming laptops get 4–7 hours of light use. If you commute, favour Ryzen models and sub-2.5kg chassis.

8. UK-Specific Tips

  • Buy from UK retailers (Amazon UK, Currys, Box) for straightforward returns under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
  • Check the keyboard layout is UK (ISO) — grey imports sometimes ship US layouts.
  • Student? Stack UNiDAYS discounts with sale prices.
  • Extended warranties: worth it on £1,500+ machines.
