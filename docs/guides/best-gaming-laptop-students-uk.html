Student Buying Checklist

  • Weight: under 2.5kg if you'll carry it daily.
  • Battery: Ryzen-based models (like the TUF A15) last longer unplugged.
  • Durability: MIL-STD tested chassis survive uni life.
  • Student discount: check UNiDAYS and manufacturer education stores — often 10% off.
  • Warranty: accidental-damage cover is worth it for a laptop that travels.
