RTX 4070 Laptop Buying Tips

  • TGP matters most: demand 140W for full performance.
  • Pair with QHD: the 4070 shines at 1440p; on 1080p panels much of its power is wasted.
  • 16GB RAM minimum, 1TB SSD — modern games are huge.
  • UK prices: expect £1,500–£1,900; below £1,500 is a genuine deal.
