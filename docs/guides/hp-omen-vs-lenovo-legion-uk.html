FAQs

Is HP Omen or Lenovo Legion better for gaming?

Legion edges it on keyboard, thermals and value. Omen is a fine choice when discounted.

Which has better UK warranty?

Lenovo's on-site options are stronger; HP's standard warranty is solid.
