What to Expect Under £1000

  • GPU: RTX 4050 is the one to get — with DLSS 3 it punches well above its weight at 1080p.
  • Display: 1080p 144Hz is standard; don't expect QHD or OLED.
  • Compromises: plasticky builds, louder fans, dimmer screens — performance is where the money goes.
  • Upgrade path: pick models with upgradeable RAM and a second SSD slot to extend lifespan.
