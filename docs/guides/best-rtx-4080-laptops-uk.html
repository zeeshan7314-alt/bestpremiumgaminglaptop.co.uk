Buying Tips

175W matters

Both our picks run the 4080 at full 175W — lower-wattage versions leave 15%+ performance on the table.

Who actually needs this?

1440p ultra high-refresh gamers, 4K gamers, and creators doing GPU rendering. Everyone else should save £1,000 with an RTX 4070.

FAQs

Is RTX 4080 overkill for 1080p?

Completely — pair it with at least a QHD 240Hz display.

RTX 4080 vs RTX 4070 laptop?

The 4080 is ~35-40% faster but costs nearly double. Worth it only for 4K or high-refresh 1440p.
